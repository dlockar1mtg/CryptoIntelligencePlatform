# Bitcoin Cycle Phase Strategic Regime Governance V1

## Governance decision

`BITCOIN_CYCLE_PHASE_STRATEGIC_REGIME_GOVERNANCE_V1`

Bitcoin cycle phase is promoted from a research hypothesis to a **limited governed strategic-regime input** for Bitcoin recommendation-policy research and forward evidence collection.

This promotion is supported by the preserved V2 historical study:

- study: `BITCOIN_FOUR_YEAR_CYCLE_EXTENDED_HISTORY_V2`
- preserved result SHA-256: `ed22cfb5eb83ac860527ca3c47c6b8d9e10a7cb3827ebb89ebd33fe8a324b9ca`
- interpretation: `PATTERN_SUPPORTED_DESCRIPTIVELY`
- completed adequately covered cycles: `3`
- refined expansion-reset ordering matches: `3`
- independent completed-cycle sample size: `3`
- small-sample warning: `TRUE`

The historical study supports promotion of cycle phase as one strategic evidence dimension. It does not establish deterministic cycle timing, causal certainty, recommendation-policy skill, or production execution authority.

## Strategic role

Cycle phase may contribute to the strategic layer that classifies Bitcoin into one of:

- `ACCUMULATE`
- `HOLD`
- `DISTRIBUTE`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`

Cycle phase is a contextual input only. It is not an executable action and it is not sufficient by itself to select any strategic state.

The current long-duration accumulation mandate remains governing. New Bitcoin capital is generally evaluated against an approximately three-year intended holding period. Existing holdings remain separate from new-capital decisions.

## Permitted cycle evidence

The strategic layer may use governed cycle-position features including:

- days since the most recent observed Bitcoin halving;
- estimated days to the next halving, with uncertainty explicitly represented before the next halving actually occurs;
- normalized position within the current halving interval;
- descriptive historical phase label;
- drawdown from prior running high;
- recovery from observed cycle trough;
- return since halving;
- historical exact 1-year, 2-year, and 3-year forward-return distributions by comparable cycle phase;
- the V2 expansion-peak -> reset-trough -> recovery structure as historical evidence.

The incomplete 2024->2028 interval must never be counted as an independent completed historical cycle.

## Required corroborating evidence

Cycle phase must be evaluated alongside other governed evidence. At minimum, strategic interpretation must consider available evidence from these categories when present:

1. drawdown and price-state context;
2. medium/long momentum and trend;
3. volatility and risk regime;
4. macro/liquidity conditions;
5. governed valuation or on-chain evidence when available and point-in-time safe enough for the intended use;
6. historical forward-return distributions with explicit small-sample uncertainty.

Missing corroborating evidence remains missing. It must not be replaced with synthetic zero, neutral, worst-case, or favorable values.

## Tactical 7-day model role

The frozen V4 7-day model retains its existing certified role as **tactical entry-timing evidence** under reconstructed-history out-of-sample validation.

The strategic cycle layer and 7-day tactical layer have different authority:

- strategic cycle evidence answers whether the environment is broadly compatible with accumulation, holding, or distribution research;
- the 7-day model may adjust the timing or aggressiveness of the next planned new-capital tranche;
- a negative 7-day forecast during a favorable strategic accumulation regime normally supports `DELAY` or reduced tranche aggressiveness, not automatic sale of existing long-duration Bitcoin;
- a positive 7-day forecast during a favorable accumulation regime may support `ACCELERATE` within an already-governed capital plan;
- the 7-day model may not override a strategic `DISTRIBUTE`, `NO_NEW_CAPITAL`, risk-reduction, or insufficient-evidence state merely because its short-term forecast is positive.

V4 model selection is not reopened by this governance decision.

## Prohibited calendar authority

No one feature, including calendar year, halving distance, or cycle phase, may independently force a BUY or SELL.

The following are explicitly prohibited:

- treating 2026 or 2027 as mandatory buying years;
- treating 2028 as a mandatory no-buy year;
- treating 2029 as a mandatory liquidation year;
- assuming the next cycle must reproduce the prior three cycles;
- converting the historical four-year pattern into autonomous execution rules;
- overriding contradictory live risk or regime evidence solely because of cycle position.

The working year labels may remain useful hypotheses:

- 2026-2027: expected accumulation/reset/recovery window;
- 2028: expected halving/transition regime;
- 2029: expected late-cycle distribution opportunity.

These labels are **not deterministic execution rules**.

## Authority granted

The following authority is granted:

`BITCOIN_CYCLE_PHASE_STRATEGIC_REGIME_INPUT_AUTHORIZED=TRUE`

This means cycle phase may be incorporated into governed strategic recommendation-policy research, scoring design, forward evidence collection, and human-readable recommendation rationale.

The following authority is not granted:

`BITCOIN_CYCLE_CALENDAR_ONLY_ACTION_AUTHORIZED=FALSE`

`BITCOIN_RECOMMENDATION_POLICY_SKILL_CERTIFIED=FALSE`

`PRODUCTION_POLICY_CHANGE_AUTHORIZED=FALSE`

`AUTONOMOUS_EXECUTION_AUTHORIZED=FALSE`

`V4_MODEL_SELECTION_REOPENED=FALSE`

## Recommendation-policy status

The historical inventory remains insufficient to certify recommendation-policy skill because existing BTC/ETH recommendation observations are sparse, concentrated in only two historical run dates, and lack matured 30/90/180-day outcomes.

Therefore the governing recommendation-policy status remains:

`BTC_ETH_RECOMMENDATION_POLICY_SKILL_NOT_CERTIFIED`

The cycle promotion changes the allowable strategic feature set for future governed policy research. It does not retroactively certify Module42 or any historical recommendation policy.

## Forward evidence requirement

Future BTC recommendation records should preserve the strategic cycle evidence available at decision time, including at minimum:

- observed halving anchor used;
- days since halving;
- estimated next-halving distance and uncertainty when applicable;
- normalized cycle position;
- descriptive cycle phase;
- drawdown from running high;
- available medium/long trend evidence;
- available macro/liquidity evidence;
- strategic state;
- tactical new-capital state;
- existing-position state;
- frozen V4 7-day forecast/confidence when available;
- original Module42 action where applicable;
- planned tranche/deployment decision;
- explicit uncertainty and missing-evidence flags.

Outcomes remain exact-calendar 7/30/90/180 days, 365 days where useful, and approximately three-year strategic outcomes when matured. Missing exact endpoints remain missing.

## Production boundary

This governance decision authorizes research and forward evidence collection only. A separate policy-validation result and separate production-promotion decision are required before any production recommendation behavior is changed.

Recommendation remains distinct from execution. No autonomous purchase or sale execution is authorized.
